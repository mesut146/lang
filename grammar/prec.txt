//Binding power, from Parser::get_prec in src/ast/parser.x. Lower binds
//looser; expr_level(0) is parse_expr and expr_level(11) is as_is, which
//covers the postfix chain, the prefix operators and as/is.
0   = += -= *= /= ^=
1   ||
2   &&
3   |
4   ^
5   &
6   == !=
7   < > <= >=        two GT tokens merge into ">>" at this level
8   <<
9   + -
10  * / %
11  as/is, postfix (.name .0 [..] ?), prefix (& ! - * ++ --)

//Every level folds left, assignment included. Only the assignment spellings
//listed at 0 exist: the lexer has no PERCENTEQ/ANDEQ/OREQ/LTLTEQ/GTGTEQ
//tokens (see Lexer.g), so `a %= b` and `a >>= b` do not parse.
